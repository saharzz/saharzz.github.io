Sahar Zangeneh, PhD

Researcher | Educator | Consultant

I am a statistician specializing in design and analysis of experimental and observational studies, including individual- and community-randomized clinical trials, complex sample surveys, and observational cohort studies. I identify and integrate diverse data sources to perform population-level inference and generate real-world evidence. My methodological work focuses on developing statistical methods for missingness and non-random selection, including cases where these depend on unobserved factors (missing not at random).

I am the Founder and Principal Consultant at Cascade Insights LLP and a Clinical Associate Professor at the University of Washington. I have extensive experience building and leading teams and conducting cross-cutting collaborations across biomedical and epidemiological domains. These span chronic and infectious diseases, neonatology, sexual and mental health, and nutrition. I work with diverse data sources—from clinical and survey data to electronic health records—and populations including children and adolescents, gender minorities, and racial and ethnic minorities.

I am an educator and communicator who uses data to tell stories of people and communities. Sometimes the data speaks for itself. Other times, sophisticated methods and external data are needed to address nuances and reveal a clearer picture. I am a skeptic by training but adaptable, unafraid of new paths, and eager to learn.
