My collaborative research spans population health in diverse biomedical and epidemiological domains, including infectious and chronic diseases, sexual health, and mental health, and how medical, social, and environmental factors affect health and well-being. Much of my research has focused on at risk and hard-to-reach populations, such as children, sexual and racial/ethnic minorities, and people living in rural communities, which involve challenges in recruitment, analysis and representation.

My methodological research centers on methods for addressing missingness and selection bias, including when missingness depends on unobserved data (missing not at random). I address these problems in the context of comparative effectiveness, causal inference, and population-level inference.
